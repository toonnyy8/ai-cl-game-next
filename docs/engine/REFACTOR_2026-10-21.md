## 8. Determinism rules (in addition to REFACTOR_2026-10 §8)

1. A file split or reorder is proven by unit-forms EQUAL (§3) with an unchanged order list, then the gates. A split never
   edits a form; headers and the `;;;; ---- ` markers are the only text that changes.
2. A reorder may move a function past its callers (one unit, late-bound) but never a macro, defconstant, defstruct,
   defcomponent or deftype past a use; `declaim`s travel with their variables; `pushnew ... *char-debug*` and
