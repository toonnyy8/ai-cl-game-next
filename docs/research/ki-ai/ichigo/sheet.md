## How to read the core moves

- **Buttons**: J = attack, Mod + J = SP, Space = Step, Mod + Space = Hoho, P = Burst, Mod + P = the awakening (DUEL_CORE §1).
- **J string (NA)**: five links, each needs a hit (or a cancel). An SP after a landed J is an **AS**. Its number comes from the press
  count, `:as-by-press (1 1 2 2 3)`: J1 / J2 > AS1, J3 / J4 > AS2, J5 > AS3. The AS writes the pointer and ends the combo.
- **NS n**: the SP from neutral under pointer n. Its segments a / b / c each go on with a fresh SP, hit or not. A J in a segment's
  gap is the **SA**: the kit's `ic-sa`, or the segment's own `:sa` (NS2's charged SAs). The SA counts as one attack press, so
  SA J2 J3 + SP is AS2.
- **AS n** has n segments, each on a fresh SP. Only the last one knocks down or launches.
- **Pointer by NS**: 1 the cleaver (heavy, NS1 = the old K string); 2 the Getsuga (two charges, then JUJISHO's wave); 3 the rush
  (RANGETSU's lunge, then TSUKI-OTOSHI). The stance (idle clip) shows the pointer. The opponent can read which NS is coming.
- **Kikon**: hold the SP that lands. Keyboard and CPU: the button must stay down 12 f after the hitstop (`*core-kikon-confirm*`).
  Then the victim is knocked 2.5 m and Ichigo dashes in to the same strike again. That hit is the Kikon (Konpaku table per form below).
  - A wave segment with `:kikon-strike` (NS2c JUJISHO, AS2b) is a Kikon if the SP is held through the wave's hit. Ichigo then
    flash-steps in (up to 40 m/s, from 12 m) for the charged X (80).
  - **The victim's attack cancels the follow-up unless he is red** (Reishi < 30 %).
- **Soul Break** (his Reishi to 0): takes base + 1 Konpaku, + 2 more if the striking pointer is 3 (cap 5). Each fighter has 9
  Konpaku; 0 loses.
- **Charge**: a charged SA's `:charge-mult` (1.4 after NS2a's SA, 1.7 after NS2b's) multiplies every hit of the string after it
  (J2..J5 and the AS). It does not multiply the SA itself or a wave.
- **The triangle** (DUEL_CORE §2): NA vs NA, first hit wins; NA vs NS cancels (both go on); SA beats NA; AS beats NS; turn moves
  (AS / SA) have super armour from f6 to the end of their startup. A wave is a hazard: it is never judged by the triangle, so it
  hits clean. Kenpachi's NS2 armour (`ken-armor`) absorbs one hit through its active frames, a wave included (damage dealt, no
  reaction: catalogue M17).
- **The Getsuga's band** for the generic CPU: `:ns-range (nil (3.6 6.6) nil)`. Under pointer 2, walking in from 3.6–6.6 m, the CPU
  shoots with `*cai-ranged-p*` 0.5 per decision, then waits 80 steps. JUJISHO's wave: 3.6 m wide, 14 m/s over 12 m, knockdown,
  150 base. It leaves at NS2c's f20, about 57 f after the first SP when the three presses are latched, and lands about 76 f after
  the first press at 6.6 m. The wave hit at 13 m in the sim (catalogue C5).

## How KESSA is entered (DUEL_CORE §6, DUEL_SUBLIMATION)

- **Mod + P** with the awakening gauge full (100), in a free state (neutral). It is refused below full (catalogue L2) and inside
  a move (L3: pressed in the J string, nothing).
- The gauge fills by damage dealt (0.035 a point), damage taken (0.049) and Konpaku lost (10.5 each). Once it is full, attacking
  piles SUBLIMATION on top; the awakening keeps half of it as a damage / speed bonus.
- The awakening heals 20 % of max Reishi (`*awaken-heal*`). KESSA is permanent, with Kikon base 3 Konpaku instead of 2.
- KESSA's own `:core`: NS1 the blood chains, NS2 the black Getsuga (the same charges / wave), NS3 the clones, AS1 the four-lash
  whip, AS2 SOGA > the black crescent, AS3 his cuts with the clones.
- The NS3 clones (one per press, then their X) replay every J and AS he starts after NS3, at x0.1, 4 / 8 f behind
  (`*clone-replay-scale*`, `*clone-replay-lags*`).
- The generic CPU awakens with 0.02 a decision once the gauge is full (`*cai-awaken-p*`).
