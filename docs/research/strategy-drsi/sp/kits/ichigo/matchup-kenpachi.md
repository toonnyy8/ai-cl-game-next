## His Shikai (NOZARASHI)

- His reach jumps: **NS1a hits at 4.5 m** (slide 3.0), J3 / J7 4.2 m, J1 2.7 m. Her NS3a now loses to his NS1a from every distance (P/Ks),
  her mid-range band (3.0-4.8 m) is his too. Only her NS vs his lone J still wins (cI at 1.6-3.2 m).
- His NOZARASHI blade cuts waves (`:projectile-cut`, a kit rule, DUEL_CORE §2; not trialled here): the Getsuga is judged against his blade like any NS hit.

## The triangle after a cancel (X/K)

- Her J1 into his NS1a (cancel): her AS1 wins against nothing / his NS1b, loses to his reactive SA; her J1's own hit lands after the
  cancel (his NS1a recovers), so J2 wins every answer (X1/K).
- Her NS1a vs his J1 (cancel): her SA at once beats his AS1 and his J2 (X2/K: SA 19 + BREAK 178); her NS1b loses to his AS1 (90 + BREAK
  180). **Her SA is the answer; her next segment is the gamble.**
- Every first hit after a cancel took 148-200 SP from the loser.

## Headlines

1. **Below 2.3 m her J loses to his** (S14 vs S9); her NS beats his lone J; his NS beats her NS. She should not start with J1 against
   him: she should answer his J with NS, his NS with nothing (out of reach) or her SA after a cancel.
2. **3.0-4.8 m is hers against the sealed form**: NS3a (4.8) and the SA-g1 lunge (3.8) reach, nothing of his does; but a timed NS1a of his
   beats the lunge (slide-in). The lunge is a whiff punish / walk-in catch, not a poke into his NS.
3. **His Shikai takes that band away** (NS1a 4.5 m): from his awakening her mid-range plan stops working.
4. **The exchange**: answer his cancel with the SA (beats AS and J2); start with J only where his NS is likely (AS1 wins vs NS1b).
5. **Gauge**: at 100 SP every route runs once; at 0 only the J string does full damage. Her NS2 charge route (360) needs 80 SP and 64 f
   standing still: a punish route, not an opener.
