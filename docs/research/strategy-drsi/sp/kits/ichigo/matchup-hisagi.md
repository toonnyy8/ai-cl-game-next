## His Bankai (ranged mode, `:bankai`)

- **His J reaches 3.4-6.2 m (dead inside 3.0, S14)**, NS1a 3.0 (360°), NS2a 4.4 (S25). Inside 3.0 m her J1 and NS1a beat his J (P/Hb: I
  at 1.6 / 2.4); from 3.2 m his J beats or cancels hers. Her NS3a beats his J and NS2a at 1.6-4.2 m and loses to his NS1a everywhere.
- The bind mode (`:bankai-bind`, J 1.85 m, NS1a a chain) was not gridded; its sheet is in `opp-sheets.md`.

## The triangle after a cancel (X/H)

- Her J1 into his NS1a at 2.0 m (his dead zone: her J1's hit is the one cancelled): her AS1 wins against nothing / NS1b and loses to his
  SA-KIRI; her J2 loses to his SA at f24 and to his NS1b (X1/H).
- Her NS1a vs his J1: her SA at once beats his J2 (19), **loses to his AS1 TSUMUJI** (4 hits, he slides 3-4 m; DUEL_CORE §2.1.1's
  exception), and her NS1b loses to it too (X2/H). **Against Hisagi's J the cancel is his exchange to lose only if he picks J2.**
- Breakthroughs took 143-200 SP every time.

## Headlines

1. **He out-ranges her from 3.4 m** (chain to 6.0, bind to 5.2, Bankai J to 6.2) **and is blind inside 3.0 m** with his chain moves. Her
   game is to live inside 3 m: there her NS beats his J and his NS1a, and only his NS2a / bind (S16 / S18) beat her NS.
2. **NS3a at 3.2-4.2 m beats his chain NS1a** (S18 < S20) and loses beyond 5 m: the lunge is her way in.
3. **The Getsuga does not zone him**: his J cancels it at 6.6 m (X3/H), and he loses nothing; her keep-away game has no edge here (the
   measured `keep` arm lost whole to his `none`: arms4/ichigo.md).
4. **TSUMUJI beats her reactive SA**: after her NS is cancelled by his J, his AS1 wins; she needs his J2 to be his habit, or to avoid the
   NS-into-his-J cancel at that distance.
5. **KESSA is her best form here**: its NS3a + SA won every grid cell against his Shikai openers (the SA's armour and lunge, 4.6 m); against his Bankai
   it holds only from 4.2 m (his NS1a, 360°, beats it closer).
