## Headlines

1. **Hisagi wins the Shikai-vs-Shikai triangle at every distance he chooses**: NS2a (S16) is faster than both of Ichigo's hitting NS openers,
   NS3a beats his J1 / NS1a, NAGI owns 5-6.3 m and 3.5-5 m against everything but the NS3a rush. His x0.8 taken is the cost.
2. **The two threats are the NS3a rush (to 5.0 m, S18) and the JUJISHO wave (12 m)**. The rush: NS2a, WA (ranged), or J1 > TSUMUJI. The
   wave: NAGI during the first 20 f of the charge at 4-6 m, or Hoho through it late; never a back Step.
3. **KESSA leaves 3.5-5.5 m to NAGI entirely**; inside 2.5 m his J1 (S8) and his clones beat Hisagi's J / NS.
