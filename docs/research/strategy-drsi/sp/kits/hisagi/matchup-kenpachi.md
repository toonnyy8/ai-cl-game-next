## His Shikai (his awakening) changes the picture

- **The lunge NS1a (S16, slide 3.0) reaches 5.7 m** and beats NAGI, NS2a and NS3a at every distance 1.5-5.5 m (T Hb x Ks0). NAGI owns only
  5.8-6.4 m.
- **Hisagi's J1 cancels the lunge at every distance up to 5.5 m** (the lunge arrives in J1's active frames), so Hisagi is the J side:
  **J1 > AS1 TSUMUJI wins** at 3.0 / 4.0 / 5.0 m, also against his SA +8 (X-d: 52 / 26, knockdown at 4.1-5.5 m). J2 instead loses to his SA.
- His Shikai J1 (S9) reaches 3.4 m: inside 3.5 m his J beats Hisagi's J; NS2a vs the lunge is a same-frame NS tie.
- KYORAN (Kb): the lunge to 4.9 m at S14, J1 S7; J1 > TSUMUJI beats the lunge too (X-d: 52 + his own 40 Bankai cost).

- Routes: from the TABA tip (4.25 m) NS2a SA-g J2s AS2ab 308, the biggest in either kit; from the J tip J1-J5 AS3abc 233 and the bind.

## The bind vs him

KAGIYOSE (S18, to 6.7 m) loses to his Shikai lunge (S16) at every distance and is cancelled by his J1. Its clash rule is the reason to be
there: **a cancel inside the bind moves 25 % of the Reishi gap** (B: 300 vs 1300 → 425 vs 1175). Behind, a bound Hisagi wants cancels
(J1 into his NS, J1 vs J1 ties); ahead, he wants none (leave with AS3: J1-J5 AS3abc 271 releases him).

## Headlines

1. **Hisagi wins the sealed matchup from 3.4 m out and loses it inside 2.6 m.** Nothing of sealed Kenpachi reaches 3.4-6.4 m; there NAGI and
   NS3a win every simultaneous press. Inside 2.6 m Kenpachi is 5 f faster with the J and 2-4 f faster with the NS.
2. **Against his awakening, J1 > TSUMUJI is the answer, not distance**: the lunge covers 1.5-5.7 m and beats every Hisagi NS, but Hisagi's
   J1 cancels it wherever it lands and TSUMUJI wins the exchange against every answer tried.
3. Hisagi's routes are long-range starters: NS1a > SA > J string > AS3 from 3.5 m (321), NS3a > YOSE > J string > AS3 from 4-5.2 m (286),
   both ending at +53.
