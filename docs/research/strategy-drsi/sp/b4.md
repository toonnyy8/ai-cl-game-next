## 2. The vacuity check (proof.py `bar`)

- Each job now reports `fire_share` (judgements where its rules fire at any weight), `in_weight` (the firing-weighted share) and `lasting`.
- **VACUOUS** = `fire_share` > 0.60: its "in-job >= 2x out-of-job" is not passed (T0 plays arm k exactly where its rules fire, so out-of-job
  is ~0 by construction; the check is only informative when the job is a situation, not most of the match).
- **LATCH** = `lasting` >= 0.95 (reported, not a bar): once on, it stays on.
- **The two suspects the lead named are under 60 %, so the rule as written passes them**: KH Hisagi `range` (v0) fires on .52 of the

- No dict key in `proof.py` shadows another (checked with `ast`): TESTS holds all 19 pre-registered bars of both coordinators (Ichigo 7,
