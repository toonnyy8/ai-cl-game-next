# Roguelike / roguelite digest for a SOUL DUEL ENDLESS fusion mode (2026-10-22)

Sources are short inline links. Where a point comes from general design knowledge and not from a page I read, it is marked (gk).

## 1. Core pillars

- **Berlin Interpretation (2008)**: high-value factors are procedural generation, permadeath, turn-based, grid, non-modal, complexity, resource management, hack-and-slash, exploration. It was written to separate Rogue/NetHack/Angband from edge cases like Diablo ([wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roguelike)). A 1v1 fighter fails most of it (real-time, no grid, no exploration). Only **permadeath, procedural runs and resource management** carry over.
- **Why permadeath exists**: Rogue's developers added it because players save-scummed. Michael Toy saw it as "giving weight to each decision", not as a way to punish ([wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roguelike)). ENDLESS already follows this: the pause has no RESTART (DUEL_ENDLESS §1).
- **Roguelite** = run-based play plus meta-progression that carries between runs. Hades' design language was "take the sting out of failure": every death moves the story or unlocks something ([Inlander](https://inlander.com/culture/hades-writer-greg-kasavin-on-how-he-made-video-game-deaths-drive-a-feel-good-story-22725237), [GameSpot](https://gamespot.com/articles/hades-changes-what-it-means-to-be-a-roguelike/1100-6483420/)).
- **The run loop**: fight → reward choice → route choice → (shop/rest/event) → escalate → boss → die or win → spend meta currency → new run. ENDLESS has fight → carry → escalate, no reward/route choice, no meta.

## 2. Run structure

- **Slay the Spire**: 3 acts × 17 floors. The node types are monster, elite (drops a relic), rest (heal OR upgrade a card), shop, event, treasure, boss. Each act's map is random but follows one template; StS2 shows the whole act up front, guarantees a rest site before the boss and treasure at the halfway point ([wiki](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Map_Locations), [StS2 guide](https://sts2.untapped.gg/guides/how-to-make-the-best-map-choices-in-slay-the-spire-2)).
- **Hades**: no map. Each chamber's exit doors **preview their reward** by icon: which god's boon, Daedalus hammer, gold/obol, health, a skull for a miniboss with better loot, or a Trial with two gods where the god you refuse attacks you ([Prima](https://primagames.com/tips/hades-door-symbols-guide-boons-artifacts)). Light branching that suits a gauntlet.
- **Monster Train**: 9 rings, a **left/right two-track choice per ring** with a preview, a boss every three levels ([Gaming Trend](https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/all-aboard-next-stop-hell-monster-train-review/), [Boss Rush](https://bossrush.net/2023/03/game-review-race-through-rings-of-hell-in-monster-train/)). A binary fork is cheap to build and easy to read.
- **Balatro**: 8 antes × (Small, Big, Boss blind). Boss blinds carry a named rule, e.g. cards disabled or fewer hands. Small and Big can be **skipped** for a tag, which costs the cash and the shop ([wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balatro)). It is a fixed cadence and the bosses are announced ahead. Skipping trades safety for tempo.

## 3. Rewards and builds

- **Rarity tiers** (Hades: Common → Rare → Epic → Heroic). The tier only scales the number, e.g. True Shot 70 vs 80 ([Prima](https://primagames.com/tips/hades-what-are-duo-boons-how-get-duo-boons)). **Duo boons** are offered only when you hold qualifying boons from both gods ([Prima](https://primagames.com/tips/hades-what-are-duo-boons-how-get-duo-boons)). The rule is that a synergy is a reward for committing, offered only when it can't be a dead pick.
- **Incremental vs build-defining**: BlazBlue Entropy Effect splits plain "+5 fire" Tactics from **Potentials**, which add commands or change how existing moves work. Potentials give the expression ceiling ([NoobFeed](https://www.noobfeed.com/reviews/blazblue-entropy-effect-x-review), [tech-gaming](https://www.tech-gaming.com/blazblue-entropy-effect-x/)). Absolum's **Rituals** (e.g. weapon on fire, time stop after a perfect parry/dodge) are found per room and gone at run end ([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/146270/absolum-review-score)).
- **Pick 1 of 3, rerolls, banish**: reroll gives a new set, banish removes one option from the run's pool. Banish has little effect in a big pool and too much if it can remove a whole category ([featurebase](https://roguepoint.featurebase.app/p/banish-and-rerolls-for-perks), [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2667120/discussions/0/828204733286065468)). A reroll returning the same category turns choice into ranking ([Steam/Hades](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1145360/discussions/2/1864993869493927612)).
- **Avoiding dead picks** (gk): gate offers on prerequisites (duo rule), weight toward what is already held, never offer something the character can't use (here: per-kit filters).
- **Curses / trade-offs**: Sifu's death counter trades max HP for damage as you age ([NME](https://www.nme.com/reviews/game-reviews/sifu-review-3155201)). Hades' Trial of the Gods makes refusing one god cost you a fight against him. Rogue Legacy traits. Balatro's skip.
- **Opt-in difficulty**: Hades' **Pact of Punishment** has 15 separate conditions with Heat ranks, e.g. Hard Labor = enemy damage, Lasting Consequences = less healing, Jury Summons = more enemies, Extreme Measures = bosses get new patterns ([Prima](https://primagames.com/gaming/hades-guide-pact-of-punishment-modifiers-heat)). **StS Ascension** is 20 cumulative steps per character, unlocked by winning (StS2 cuts it to 10) ([HGG](https://www.highgroundgaming.com/slay-the-spire-ascension-guide/), [allthings](https://allthings.how/slay-the-spire-2-ascension-explained-every-level-and-how-it-differs-from-the-original/)). **Balatro stakes** stack cumulatively. **Monster Train covenants** go to 25. Risk of Rain 2 scales by **time** (Drizzle 0.5× clock, Monsoon 1.5×) ([RoR2 wiki](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Difficulty)). Dead Cells **Boss Cells** add **Malaise**, a meter against passivity, which players criticise because it keeps rising after a room is cleared ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/588650/discussions/10/2967272951905627986)).

## 4. Meta-progression

- **Unlocks (sidegrades, new options) vs permanent stats**: players widely prefer unlocks. Stat stacking is "a difficulty slider that starts too hard and ends too easy", and in Rogue Legacy upgrading the castle was close to mandatory, i.e. a grind ([QT3](https://forum.quartertothree.com/t/the-action-roguelite-thread/148031?page=2), [bugnet](https://bugnet.io/blog/how-to-design-a-roguelite-meta-progression)).
- **The accessibility answer is opt-in and transparent**: Hades **God Mode** gives 20 % damage resistance, +2 % per death, capped at 80 %, and is toggled in settings ([Can I Play That](https://caniplaythat.com/2021/08/11/hades-god-mode-explained-by-supergiant-games)).
- **Knowledge as progression** (gk; Spelunky, StS): the real meta-gain is the player learning matchups, which is exactly what a fighting game trains. Unlock **options** (new boons in the pool, new start variants, Heat levels), not raw power.

## 5. Fighting-game and action precedents

- **MK11 Towers of Time** (2019): modifiers hit both sides, there were unblockables, and bosses had up to 4000 HP. It was accused of pushing purchases. NRS admitted "AI difficulty and modifier aggressiveness was tuned too high", cut boss HP to 1500, made modifiers trigger less, made unblockables blockable, and raised rewards ([TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/netherrealm-towers-of-time-fix-details/), [MP1st](https://mp1st.com/news/mortal-kombat-11-towers-of-time-upcoming-changes-outlined-by-netherrealm)).
- **MK1 Invasions**: a board map, levelled characters, elemental talismans, "Elite" fighters with modifiers. Critics found it "fun but tedious". Players hated the vision-blocking smoke stages and the poor rewards ([Gamer Guides](https://gamerguides.com/mortal-kombat-1/review), [GGRecon](https://ggrecon.com/reviews/mortal-kombat-1-review)).
- **Smash Ultimate World of Light / Spirits**: every fight is a themed **condition battle**, e.g. a CPU that only taunts, or one that favours certain attacks. Primary spirits form a triangle (attack > grab > shield > attack) and support spirits fill 0–3 slots. Some conditions have a matching counter-spirit ([MarioWiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Spirit_(Super_Smash_Bros._Ultimate)), [SSBWiki](https://www.ssbwiki.com/Condition)). Model: one readable modifier per fight, with a counter.
- **Killer Instinct Shadow Lords** (2016): a team of 3, procedural scenarios against "mimic" fighters and **Guardians** that add mechanics like parry or armor. Previews called it complex ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killer_Instinct_(2013_video_game)), [ultra-combo](https://www.ultra-combo.com/shadow-lords-mode/)).
- **BlazBlue Entropy Effect**: proof that a fighting IP can work as an action roguelite. The criticisms are long reading text and combos less precise than the fighters ([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/147828/blazblue-entropy-effect-x-review-score)).
- **Absolum** (Oct 2025, OpenCritic 86): ~30-minute runs "perfectly paced", the core moveset fixed with rituals on top, and meta currency for moves and a skill tree. Some critics say the roguelite layer doesn't serve the beat-'em-up and it lacks variance ([OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/19143/absolum), [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/146270/absolum-review-score)).
- **Sifu**: the death counter / aging works as a failure resource **inside** the run, and killing a key enemy lowers it ([NME](https://www.nme.com/reviews/game-reviews/sifu-review-3155201)).

## 6. Pitfalls and principles

- **Run length**: Absolum's ~30 min is praised. A fighter at ~2–3 min per stage gives **8–12 stages ≈ 25–35 min** for a full run.
- **Fair failure**: show the threat before you commit (Hades doors, Balatro boss preview, Monster Train track preview). Modifiers should be **telegraphed, constant per fight, symmetric or clearly labelled**, and never block vision (MK1 smoke) or remove core defence (MK11 unblockables).
- **Seeded / daily**: StS's Daily Climb gives one attempt with fixed seed and modifiers, the same for everyone ([wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slay_the_Spire)). This needs a deterministic sim, which SOUL DUEL already has.
- **Determinism and replays**: keep reward RNG separate from fight RNG (ENDLESS's bag LCG already avoids `sim-rnd`), so the offers can't be manipulated by how you fight.
- **Readability in a fast game**: ≤ 3 active run modifiers at a glance, icons plus one line, and no boon whose effect needs a frame-data reading. BBEE's walls of text are a warning.
- **Touch UI**: 3 large cards in the thumb zone, ~1 s input lock (ENDLESS's STAGE CLEAR already does this), tap to inspect, tap again to take, and the run's build always one tap away.

## Mapped onto SOUL DUEL ENDLESS

1. **Pick 1 of 3 "Reishi boons" at STAGE CLEAR** (alongside CONTINUE/REVERT). *Why*: this is the core roguelite hook, and it fits the existing `:clear` screen and the carry plist. *Risk*: every boon must stay out of the native/browser hash-parity path except as data, and balance spans 7 kits.
2. **Two boon layers: generic stat (Reishi +10 %, guard regen, flash-step cost) and kit-defining "Potentials"** that change one move per character (BBEE). *Why*: builds come from Potentials. *Risk*: the Potentials cost content per character, and the drawn reach must still equal the hit reach.
3. **Door-style route preview: a binary fork per stage** (Monster Train / Hades): "opponent X + rare boon" vs "opponent Y + heal Konpaku 2". *Why*: real choices for little UI. *Risk*: the bag's "each once per bag" guarantee needs a rule for forks.
4. **Elite / boss cadence**: every 3rd stage an "elite" (awakened at FIGHT, the ramp's existing knob) with a better boon tier; stage 9/12 a boss with a named rule (Balatro boss blind). *Why*: reuses `*endless-ramp*`. *Risk*: the boss rules must be readable and not hide fighters.
5. **Konpaku as the currency**: spend Konpaku at a rest/shop node for a boon, or trade one away for a stronger "cursed" boon (Sifu / Trial of the Gods). *Why*: it makes the existing 9-cap carry economy meaningful. *Risk*: a death spiral, which needs a floor (never below 1 by choice).
6. **Duo boons gated on holding two element/form tags** (fire + ice, Bankai + flash). *Why*: synergy with no dead picks. *Risk*: the combinatorics, so start with 3–5 duos.
7. **Opponent modifiers in Smash-spirit style**: one per stage, constant, shown on the INTRO card ("CPU favours ranged", "P2 starts awakened", "low gravity flash step"). *Why*: variety from existing CPU knobs and gauges with no new AI. *Risk*: MK11's lesson: no unblockables, no random triggers.
8. **Heat / Pact ladder per character** as the long-term difficulty (StS Ascension style, unlocked by clearing stage 10): opponent Reishi %, fewer boon choices, Konpaku +1 instead of +2. *Why*: replaces stat meta-progression, and the record becomes "stage at Heat N". *Risk*: the localStorage record slots need a new key scheme.
9. **Meta-progression = unlocks only**: new boons enter the pool, starting relic variants, the Heat levels. No permanent stats, plus an opt-in God-Mode-like ASSIST row. *Why*: avoids grind, and knowledge is the real progression in a fighter. *Risk*: players expect visible growth, so show a collection screen.
10. **Seeded daily run**: a fixed seed, the boons and modifiers from the seed, one attempt, the score shared as a code. *Why*: the sim is already deterministic and the bag is pure. *Risk*: no online board (YAGNI), so this is just a seed share.
11. **Run length target 8–10 stages + boss ≈ 25–35 min**, with an "ENDLESS after the boss" continuation for the existing record. *Why*: a roguelite needs an ending. *Risk*: the autopilot gate (80990) needs a new pacing window.
12. **Gate tooling**: extend the autopilot with a boon-pick policy (random / greedy) and report clear rate per character × Heat. *Why*: finds dominant boons early (like assistgate). *Risk*: the gate's runtime grows with boons × characters.
