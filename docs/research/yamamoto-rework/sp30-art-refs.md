Images are in the git-ignored `.refs/yamamoto-sp30/` (Bleach / Demon Slayer / Berserk fandom wikis and Wikimedia Commons, through their

What makes it read:
- The canon skeletons are **flat black or dark-red silhouettes with a pale outline and a few rib lines**, not detailed bones; a mass of them
  reads as a texture, one near skull is the scale cue (ch. 508's foreground skull is about a tenth of the frame).
- The mound in ep. 372 is built from **open reaching hands and skull circles on a slope**: a slope of overlapping skulls plus raised hands is
  enough to read as "climbing on each other"; use the slope angle (about 50-60 degrees) for the tower's lean before it topples.
- Ch. 509 shows the break: **a narrow pile, then thin shards and a few whole skeletons flying off its edge**; for the shatter, a fan of
  whole small skeletons with a few loose bones is clearer than dust.
- The cyan star glyph (a six-armed asterisk) marks the technique in both ch. 508 and ep. 372: a cheap, readable accent over the pile.

What makes it read:
- Pompeii casts: the target should **keep its silhouette and lose its face and detail**, then fall; a flat grey fill with the pose intact
  reads as "turned to ash" better than particles alone.
- Cinders are **short curved streaks with a few bright points**, glow behind them; falling slowly, not an explosion.

## 3. 西 flame trailing the sword swing

| File | Source URL | What it shows | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ros_yamamoto_ryujin_jakka.jpg` | bleach.fandom.com/wiki/File:RoS_Yamamoto_Ryujin_Jakka.png | RoS: Yamamoto low-angle, the blade drawn through a fan of fire, three yellow spiral arcs around the sword arm, flame pillars behind | B |
| `ds_rengoku_rising_scorching_sun.jpg` | demonslayer.fandom.com/wiki/File:Rising_Scorching_Sun.png | manga: a full circular flame arc following a rising cut, flame edge hooked and curling at the tip, blade at the arc's end | C |
| `ds_rengoku_flame_tiger.jpg` | demonslayer.fandom.com/wiki/File:Flame_Tiger.png | manga: the swing becomes one big beast-shaped flame mass thrown ahead of the blade | C |
| `ds_rengoku_unknowing_fire.jpg` | demonslayer.fandom.com/wiki/File:Unknowing_Fire.png | manga: a straight lunge, the flame a narrow tapering cloud behind the body | C |
| `sheet_ds_hinokami_blazing_universe.jpg` | demonslayer.fandom.com/wiki/File:Hinokami_Chronicles_-_Third_Form-_Blazing_Universe.gif | game: a tall vertical flame column rises at the hit point; the fighter is already past it | B |

What makes it read:
- The flame is **the sweep's path**: it starts at the blade and widens away from it, like the RoS spiral arcs; the thin edge is at the
  blade tip and the mass trails behind it (Rising Scorching Sun, RoS).
- Hooked, curling tips on the flame edge (Rengoku's hooks, ch. 508's tongues) give the 西 flames the same family look as the mantle.
- A forward lunge needs a **short tapering tail** (Unknowing Fire), a wide arc needs a **ring** (Rising Scorching Sun): match the
  shape to the move.

## 4. Shikai close range: 120 degree fire arc, close ring or dome, 焱熱地獄 floor disc

| File | Source URL | What it shows | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sheet_ros_yamamoto_awakening.jpg` | bleach.fandom.com/wiki/File:RoS_Genry%C5%ABsai_Shigekuni_Yamamoto_Awakening.gif | RoS: face close-up, then flames rising round the feet in a ring with a thin orange arc swung above it, then the sword drawn through flame | B |
| `commons_kilauea_fissure_curtain.jpg` | commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kilauea_eastern_rift_zone_fissure_eruption_May_2018.jpg | a line of ground fire with spark fountains rising 2-3 times the height of the palm trees | D |
| `commons_fire_whirl.jpg` | commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fire_whirl_(FWS).jpg | a twisting thin flame column standing on a flat bed of low flames | D |
| `ds_rengoku_rising_scorching_sun.jpg` | (above) | a 120-180 degree flame arc as a crescent in front of the body | C |

Canon Ennetsu Jigoku pillars are already in `.refs/yamamoto/`; the Taimatsu / Jōkaku Enjō frames there stay the base for the wall.

What makes it read:
- A **floor disc** reads as fire when it is a **bed of low flames with taller tongues spaced unevenly** and sparks rising from the
  whole area (Kilauea, fire whirl base), not a flat ring.
- RoS shows the ring as **flames at the feet plus a thin bright arc above**, so a ring around him needs a bright rim line to be seen
  against a bed of fire.
- The 120 degree arc: draw it as a crescent whose outer edge is bright and whose body is translucent (Rising Scorching Sun's
  white-bright edge), so the opponent stays visible through it.

| `ds_rengoku_flame_tiger.jpg`, `ds_rengoku_unknowing_fire.jpg` | (above) | the finishing form is the biggest flame of the set and is thrown past the body | C |

Honest state: no usable still of an old swordsman's or iaido finishing cut or of an RoS combo ender could be fetched (the one kendo
photo tried showed nothing the existing J1-J3 notes lack, and was dropped). The rest of this item rests on `motion-refs.md` and design
inference (D):
- A heavier ender is a **bigger arc plus a stop**: the blade ends with the body fixed and the flame still moving, as in the Flame
  Tiger page.
- The finisher should **differ in the plane** from J1-J3 (lunge, rising backhand, thrust): a vertical downward cut or a wide
  horizontal sweep gives a fourth shape.
