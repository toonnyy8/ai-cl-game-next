# Genryūsai Shigekuni Yamamoto: motion references for the strike-clip rebuild

Research pass 2026-10-13. Use: rebuilding Yamamoto's keyframed clips in SOUL DUEL (Shikai, Hellfire, Bankai East / West,
South, North) so that each strike has its own silhouette and whole-body motion. Method: skill `duel-motion-style`;
format: `docs/research/kenpachi-rework/motion-refs.md`. Looks: `canon-looks.md` (same folder).

**Fetch note.** bleach.fandom.com page views answer 403; its MediaWiki API (`api.php?action=parse&prop=wikitext`) gave the
wikitext of the character page, the battle pages (vs Shunsui & Ukitake, vs Wonderweiss, vs Driscoll Berci, vs Royd
Lloyd, vs Yhwach), the technique pages and `The Fire (episode)`. RoS came from the official JP page (bn-ent.net) and the
Resource of Souls data-mine (in-game EN strings). The wiki hosts **14 short anime clips as GIFs** (190×108, ~20 fps); I
read them as frame sheets at 0.5–0.8 s, so they give poses and order, not exact timing. No full episode was watched.

**Grades.**
- **A**: canon as told by the wiki's battle pages and chapter-cited prose (they follow the manga panel by panel), or seen by
  me in a manga-colour still or an anime GIF (file named; anime-only details are marked "anime").
- **B**: official game text or art (RoS JP page / in-game strings, Brave Souls).
- **C**: secondary sources (fan wikis on old games, reviews).
- **D**: my inference or proposal. A design proposal, not a reference.

**A correction to the brief.** Ittō Kasō is from the Aizen fight (ch. 395–396). Against Driscoll Berci (ch. 503–504) he
uses one Shikai slash.

## 1. Canon: how he fights, fight by fight

### 1.1 General (wiki Powers; A)
- "Even with a single hand, few could hope to face him … his powerful skill can take down most with a **single precise
  strike**" (ch. 338, ch. 155). Kubo: "Imperturbable and like an **unmovable pivot**" (Bootleg). He wins by economy, not
  by flurries.
- At peace he slouches, eyes as slits, leaning on the cane; asleep standing once. In battle he opens his eyes, straightens,
  and when serious **strips to the waist**.
- Flame obeys the blade: Ryūjin Jakka "incinerates anything … Yamamoto waves the sword at"; he controls which targets burn
  and how hot (ch. 339) — a wave of the sword is enough.
- Hakuda master: Ikkotsu (one punch), Sōkotsu (two fists), catches a giant's strike one-handed (anime ep. 226), tears off
  limbs bare-handed (ch. 394). Shunpo master: he outruns Shunsui and Ukitake and is **already sitting there waiting** (ch.
  154). Kidō without incantation (Ittō Kasō, ch. 395).
- He is a **trapper**: the whole Fake Karakura defence was set up to catch Aizen in Ennetsu Jigoku (ch. 393, Aizen: "crafty").
- He **takes hits on purpose**: Aizen's sword through his gut, then he grabs the arm (ch. 393); Driscoll's lightning while he
  stands still (ch. 504); Wonderweiss's hundred-arm barrage "with mere scratches" (ch. 394).

### 1.2 vs Shunsui and Ukitake, Soul Society arc (ch. 154–156, anime ep. 55–56; A)
- He is **sitting** behind them when they arrive, then stands up (ch. 154 pp. 17–19): the idle and the intro beat.
- He **stares** Nanao down: reiatsu through a look alone paralyses her (ch. 155).
- He **peels the wood off the cane** to reveal the sheathed katana (ch. 155 pp. 8–11; `155Yamamoto reveals`).
- They cross blades with him and he **pushes both back at once** (ch. 155 pp. 11–14).
- He **removes his haori and top**, then releases: the blade becomes fire that coils around him; the heat eats the clouds
  (ch. 155 pp. 22–24, ch. 156 pp. 6–9). He walks toward them: "what are they waiting for".
- The clash is an explosion of fire; the fight is off-panel; he comes out unhurt (ch. 156, 172).

### 1.3 Ayon and the Tres Bestias, Fake Karakura (ch. 337–339; anime ep. 225–226; A)
- **Onibi**: "takes his Zanpakutō in its sealed cane form and thrusts it at his opponent, punching a large hole in their
  body" (ch. 337 p. 18). Anime (`Onibi.gif` 5.5–8 s): he walks past, the cane held low in the right hand, a short jab; the
  hole appears after.
- **Catches Ayon's strike one-handed** and overpowers it (anime ep. 226).
- **Hitotsume: Nadegiri** (ch. 338 pp. 11–15): a cut "so precise and swift, its victims are not even aware of it until
  after". Anime (`HitotsumeNadegiri.gif`): the cane disintegrates to the blade (1.6 s); his face, eyes shut; the cut is
  never shown; he is seen from behind **slowly lowering / sheathing** (9.6–16.8 s); a beat later Ayon's head **splits
  down the middle** (19–22 s).
- **Taimatsu** (ch. 338 p. 17–20): "a great inferno with a simple wave". Manga colour (`338Taimatsu`): one-armed sweep, the
  body twisting into it, the haori flaring. Anime (`Taimatsu.gif`): **the eye opens** (0.5 s, the tell), the draw, one
  wave, the flame becomes a tornado around the target; then his back, the haori billowing.
- Tres Bestias attack from above: he "takes them down by swinging his Shikai", "lightly toasts" them (ch. 339); `339Taimatsu2`
  shows **a semicircle arch of flame over his head**.

### 1.4 vs Aizen and Wonderweiss (ch. 393–396; anime ep. 294; A)
- Arrives "in a roaring column of fire" behind Aizen (ch. 393).
- **Lets Aizen stab him through the stomach, grins, grabs Aizen's arm** — "I've got you" — and **Ennetsu Jigoku** rises:
  fire "running down the length of the cutting edge … into the ground", circles, pillars (ch. 393 pp. 6–14).
- Swings at Wonderweiss, who **catches the blade**, seals the flames and **throws him** into the city (ch. 393).
- **Ikkotsu**: one punch through Wonderweiss's abdomen, sending him through several buildings (ch. 394 pp. 5–9).
  `394Yamamoto reveals`: right fist level at shoulder height, arm straight, sleeve pushed up, the body behind it.
- Shunpo to Aizen and a punch (intercepted). **Removes his top** ("this will hurt a little").
- **Leaps** clear of a punch; a tendril comes at him: he **grabs and tears it off**; caught by both arms, he takes the
  hundred-arm barrage, then **rips both arms off** (ch. 394 pp. 10–17; `394Yamamoto rips`: both hands overhead, legs
  splayed in the air).
- **Sōkotsu** after Shunpo in front: two fists side by side, arms locked straight, the body leaning in (ch. 394 pp. 18–19;
  `394Sokotsu`, `Sōkotsu.gif` 20–28 s). Wonderweiss shatters.
- **Leaps onto the swelling head** and smothers the explosion with his body; lies burnt in a crater (ch. 395).
- From the ground **grabs Aizen's ankle**: "don't make me repeat myself", and casts **Ittō Kasō** through his charred arm,
  "his arm cracking as he casts it": a red pillar shaped like a katana tip (ch. 395–396; `396Itto Kaso1`, `IttōKasō.gif`).

### 1.5 vs Driscoll Berci (ch. 503–504; anime ep. 5 = Ep371; A)
- Appears in front of Hisagi and **deflects a javelin Heilig Pfeil with his hand** (ch. 503 pp. 12–17; `503Yamamoto deflects`:
  the back of the right hand up, small and still).
- Struck again and again by the stolen Bankai's lightning, he **stands motionless, eyes closed** in sorrow; then "simply
  … walking forward" (ch. 504 pp. 1–14).
- **Shout** (eyes wide, mouth open in the beard), release, and **one slash**: a blast of flame strips Driscoll to the bone
  (ch. 504 pp. 14–17; `YamamotoIncineratesDriscoll.gif` 0.8–4 s: the shout close-up, then the right arm thrown out straight
  ahead with the fire).
- Takes off: "bursts into the air in a scorching trail of flame", "hurtling across the Seireitei like a comet" (ch. 504–505;
  `505Yamamoto takes off`).

### 1.6 vs Royd Lloyd as "Yhwach", Shikai then Bankai (ch. 505–509; Ep372 "The Fire"; A)
Shikai:
1. **Slams into the ground** behind Royd (ch. 505 pp. 17–20); anime: Shunpo snatches Kenpachi out of his grip.
2. Three Sternritter drop from above: **engulfed in one explosion of fire** without a visible swing (ch. 506 pp. 4–10).
3. **Cracks his neck and right shoulder**, then a slash cuts Royd's forearm through Blut Vene (ch. 506 pp. 10–12).
4. **Rushes in**: Royd leans back, the blade passes **over his nose** (a level horizontal cut at face height).
5. Royd kicks; Yamamoto **blocks the leg with the sword** and is sent flying onto a roof; he **leaps back**, Royd throws
   roof tiles, he **repels them with flames** back at Royd.
6. "**Comes down on him with a spinning vertical slash that cuts through the entire building**" (ch. 506).
7. **Already standing next to Royd** out of the dust, "sword held ready to slash"; Royd's Reishi sword cuts the flames.

Bankai:
8. The flames **vanish into the blade**; the charred katana (ch. 506 pp. 13–17).
9. **Rushes forward and slashes**: Royd evades back, only the cloak is cut (ch. 507). `507Yamamoto attacks`: a deep lunge,
   right foot far forward, rear leg straight, the right arm locked out level, the blade horizontal at the target, the
   torso tipped forward.
10. A **downward slash** misses Royd's face and the **tip strikes the ground**: a chunk of ground and the building behind are
    erased (ch. 507 pp. 8–11). Anime (`…Higashi….gif`): a low rushing step with a fire burst behind (12 s); the blade raised
    high, angled, one-handed (14.4–16 s); a forward lunge, head low, blade trailing (16.8 s); the blade descends
    diagonally and the tip just touches the floor at Royd's feet (19–21.6 s); the chasm (22.4–24 s).
11. Anime: Royd **throws and kicks three chunks of rubble**; he cuts them all; they **trade dodged blows** and land on opposite
    ends of a roof; he **holds the blade up level** to name East (`507Zanka no Tachi, East`); then a downward slash that
    **strikes the roof edge** when Royd sidesteps (Ep372).
12. Royd's Reishi sword hits him and **is erased**; he is unhurt — West, "impossible to even touch" (ch. 507 pp. 14–17).
    Flames **circle his feet and enshroud him**; the ground melts, **his feet sink in**; he **approaches** slowly (ch. 508).
13. Heilig Pfeil: he **deflects it** (manga) / **casually cuts it in two halves that explode behind him** (anime); he
    **leaps** to attack (ch. 508 pp. 8–9).
14. **South**: "embeds Zanka no Tachi into the ground several meters away"; skeletons crawl out of the ruptured earth;
    anime: blade held **upright, point down, before his face** during the incantation, then **driven straight down**
    (`…Minami.gif` 8.8–13.6 s) (ch. 508 pp. 14–17).
15. Skeletons **wall him off** from Royd's leap; he **turns and walks away**, his back to Royd: "come at me when you are
    through them" (ch. 509 pp. 6–9).
16. **North**: flame gathers on the blade; "a blazing slash" — an enormous wave of fire takes Royd's forearm and torso
    (ch. 509 pp. 12–16). Manga colour: a broad diagonal band, the blade a smear trailing behind him.
17. Releases the Bankai; rain; **turns and walks away** (ch. 509 p. 17).

### 1.7 vs the real Yhwach (ch. 510–511; Ep372–373; A)
- Startled, he **turns** to find Yhwach behind him; when he re-releases, the Bankai is stolen.
- Yhwach offers to raise the dead: Yamamoto **leaps at him in rage**, then **stops in stunned silence** at the broadsword.
- **Cut diagonally through from the left shoulder** to the waist; still "grabs hold of his cloak / leg with his remaining
  arm" (ch. 511 pp. 4–9). Grabbing never stops — the same hand-grab as vs Aizen.

### 1.8 Young Yamamoto (ch. 504, 511; anime ep. 5–7; A / C)
- Text only: "a demon with a sword who would use anything and everything to win" (Yhwach, ch. 511); "Eijisai" then
  "Jūjisai" for the scars (ch. 504). He **challenged Chōjirō to defeat him** and took the Bankai standing, gaining a scar.
- Anime overlays his younger self as Yhwach sees him in a rage and when West appears (Ep372). Posture from the stills:
  upright, square, chin down, a glare (C). No young-self fight choreography exists in canon beyond silhouettes.

## 3. Archetypes and the choice

| Archetype | Canon / game examples | What makes it read |
|---|---|---|
| **The unmovable pivot** (economy, iai) | Nadegiri, "single precise strike", Onibi (ch. 337–338) | almost no wind-up, one snap, then stillness; the result arrives late (a delayed split) |
| **The flame wave** (one wave = an inferno) | Taimatsu, the Sternritter burst, RoS Homuranagi | a wide one-armed sweep with the whole torso turning, the haori flaring; the fire outruns the blade |
| **The point** (pointing the blade, fire along it) | Jōkaku Enjō, Kyokujitsujin's tip | the arm locked out level, the body still behind it, the line of the blade = the line of the attack |
| **The fencer's lunge** | the rushing cut vs Royd, `507Yamamoto attacks` | a very deep one-armed lunge, the torso tipped forward over the front knee, the target leaning back |
| **The hammer from the sky** | the spinning vertical slash; the comet landing; the tip-tap | a body turn or leap feeding one vertical drop; the impact on the floor |
| **The brawler-sage** | Ikkotsu, Sōkotsu, arm-ripping, the grab | straight-line punches with the body behind them; grabs that hold the enemy in place |
| **The immovable furnace** | Driscoll's lightning; West; Aizen's stab | he does not move while hit; the counter comes from standing still |

**The choice (proposal, D, for the user to approve):** Shikai = **the flame wave** for K (wide sweeps that throw fire, with the
spinning vertical as the finale) and **the unmovable pivot** for J (small snaps, then the burning sleeve as the finale);
Hellfire = the same strings with the **bared torso** and Nadegiri as the pivot's purest form; Bankai East = **the
fencer's lunge** for J and **the hammer** for K (tip to the ground as the finale); West = **the immovable furnace**; South /
North = **the point** (plant) and **the hammer from the sky** (the passing band). Punches (Ikkotsu) stay the Breaker in
every form.

## 5. Motion bars for SOUL DUEL

Rule: **no two strikes in a form share an arc plane and a hand set**. Yamamoto is one-armed (right) in TYBW, so the hand
sets are: **R-blade forehand**, **R-blade backhand**, **R-point** (thrust / point-down), **R-fist**, **L-sleeve** (the
empty sleeve / stump), **body** (no strike plane: aura, stamp). Planes: horizontal R→L, horizontal L→R, diagonal ↘ (high
right to low left, his view), diagonal ↗ (low to high), vertical down, vertical up, thrust (level), point-down. One
archetype per string, with a finale. The slot column follows the current kit; **read `:arc` / `:vol` first** (§4).

### 5.1 Shikai (Ryūjin Jakka, flame on the blade)

**Idle.** The old man's slouch: head forward, eyes slit, the right hand on the hilt-end resting the point on the ground in
front like the cane (A: the cane carry, RoS art), the empty left sleeve hanging, weight on both heels. A **neck-and-
shoulder crack** as the idle variation (A ch. 506). **Walk.** Slow, unhurried, upright from the hips, the blade low at the
right side; he never hurries toward an enemy (A ch. 504, 508). **Run.** Not a run: a gliding Shunpo stride, upper body
still, a flame trail behind (A: the comet ch. 505; B: RoS Enmatoi "rush forward wreathed in flames"). **Intro.** Sitting
or leaning on the cane, one eye opens, the wood peels, draw (A).

## 6. Gaps and how to close them
- **Shikai J / K choreography is thin in canon.** Canon gives single strikes (Nadegiri, Taimatsu, the Royd slashes), never a
  three-hit string. S1–S2, S5, E3–E6 and W3 are **D proposals** built on canon manner. Close: the user approves or replaces
  them; the RoS character trailer (`https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2JYfn-o37aA`) and RoS Q / F strings in a gameplay video
  would give a B-grade bar for the J / K strings.
- **Exact anime timing** (the Royd fight's rush, the tip-tap, Tenchi Kaijin): the GIFs are 20 fps and cropped. Close: watch
  Ep372 at full frame rate and log the frames per beat (wind-up / S / hold).
- **North's body**: the manga shows the band, not the pose; the anime hides it in effects. B2's passing cut is D. Close:
  ch. 509 pp. 15–16 in black-and-white.
- **Which hand Ikkotsu used** (two arms then): the stills show the right fist; the game has only the right arm, so it holds.
  **Sōkotsu** (two fists) **cannot be done one-armed**: drop it, or use it only in a pre-TYBW flashback.
- **The sig's three beats** have no canon order; S7 combines the flame arch (A), the coil (A) and RoS's halo (B). Approve
  or replace.
- **Measure before drawing**: the East caps are thin lines on the facing axis (§4); each E-strike's drawn tip must cross the
  centre line at S (FK ±0.15 m). The borrowed Shikai clips in East and TENCHI should be replaced by their own clips (skill
  red flag).
