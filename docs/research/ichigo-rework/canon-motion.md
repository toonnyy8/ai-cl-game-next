# Kurosaki Ichigo (TYBW): motion references for the strike-clip rebuild

Research pass 2026-10-14. Use: rebuilding Ichigo's keyframed clips in SOUL DUEL (the Shikai with two blades, KESSA with
the Bankai) so each strike has its own silhouette and whole-body motion. Looks, sources, method, episode numbering and the
image table: `canon-looks.md` (same folder). Format follows `docs/research/yamamoto-rework/motion-refs.md`.

**Fetch note.** Everything "wiki" comes through the bleach.fandom.com MediaWiki API: the battle pages follow the manga
panel by panel and cite chapter and episode; the episode pages (Ep411–Ep413) describe the anime shot by shot. The wiki's
GIFs are 190×108 at ~20 fps; I read them as frame sheets (0.15–1.4 s), so they give poses and order, not exact timing.
No full episode was watched.

**Grades.** **A** canon (wiki battle/episode page with a cite, or a still/GIF I looked at: file named; "anime" marks
anime-only). **B** official game text/art (RoS JP page and in-game names via the wiki's Other Media, Brave Souls).
**C** secondary. **D** my inference or proposal. **U** a decision the user already made.

**Corrections to the brief.** He never fights Haschwalth with two blades (Haschwalth cuts the *old* Bankai, ch. 514). The
Getsuga Jūjishō vs Candice is TYBW **ep. 21** (Ep387; "Part 2 ep. 8" in the fact file is the same episode by cour count).
In the anime the 血鎖 fight is ep. 45 only; ep. 46 opens with Yhwach saying that future "never existed".

## 1. Canon: how he fights, fight by fight

### 1.1 General (wiki Powers; A)
- "While usually fighting right-handed, Ichigo is ambidextrous … Since attaining the true form of Zangetsu, he has displayed
  great skill at wielding both blades at once" (ch. 583 p. 8, 619 p. 5, 673 p. 13). **Long blade right, short blade left**
  in every still I looked at (`542…`, `Ep394IchigoPreparesFight`, `Ep394YhwachEvadesIchigo`, `ros_tybw_img_ill.webp`).
- **Forward grip on both** (see `canon-looks.md` §1). The short blade is **pointed** at the enemy (Ep409, RoS 朔望).
- He often **opens with the long blade alone and draws the short one mid-fight** (Ep408: "draws the smaller sword of
  Zangetsu as well"; Ep412: "pulls out the smaller blade to slash"; Ep413: "draws his other sword and strikes"); he
  **sheathes** it to go one-sword (ch. 676).
- **Small Getsuga from the short blade** "by simply pointing his smaller sword at the enemy and making a small slash"
  (ch. 583 pp. 10–11); the big one from the long blade (ch. 618 pp. 15–17).
- **Getsuga Jūjishō**: "a Getsuga Tenshō with each blade … fuses them into a large, cross-shaped blast (normally with the
  smaller blade first, then the larger blade last)"; manga: he "crosses the path of his sword swings" (ch. 583 pp. 15–17);
  the Bambies page: "slashes his two swords through the air in an X-formation". Gold in manga colour (`583Getsuga_Jujisho`).
- **Chain** (チェイン): calls either blade back to his hand "with a simple gesture, akin to pulling it by an invisible chain"
  (Ep406; `Chain406.gif` 5.2 s: open left palm, the short blade flies into it).
- **Shunpo** without the old Bankai; Wahrwelt (no Reishi footholds): "repeatedly outmaneuvering and dodging" on foot (Ep409).
- Bare-handed: grabs and **throws** Sternritter, **punches** three in a row, holds Golden Crunch apart (ch. 582, Ep387);
  **grabs the wrist** of the enemy's sword arm with his left hand while the right cuts (ch. 618, ch. 676).
- **Blut Vene** lets him take a hit (Uryū's arrow, Ep396).

### 1.2 The reforging (ch. 538–542; anime ep. 13 = Ep379; A)
- He **reaches into the forge's fire and grabs** the blade while it is being forged; "after a burst of heat, Ichigo stands
  with his new Zanpakutō, which is now comprised of two distinct blades" (ch. 542 pp. 1–14; `Ep379IchigoGrabsBlade`:
  silhouetted in a wall of flame).
- The reveal pose (`542Ichigo_s_Shikai__Zangetsu`, `Ep379IchigoShikaiZangetsu`): standing tall, seen from behind and below,
  **the long blade held straight out to the side** at shoulder height in the right hand, **the short blade hanging
  point-down** from the relaxed left arm. Torn, sleeveless top. An intro / awakening pose.

### 1.3 Return to the Seireitei: the Bambies and Candice (ch. 581–584; ep. 21 = Ep387; A)
- Arrives at Kenpachi's side; Candice drops on him: he **grabs her arm and throws** her into a tower, then **punches**
  Liltotto, Meninas and Giselle into buildings (his cape snagged) (ch. 582 pp. 2–5).
- Walks out of the 5-gigajoule Galvano Blast unhurt; the Ōken clothes burn off to the X outfit; **draws both blades** and
  "parried every Heilig Pfeil" (ch. 582–583).
- Candice attacks with two wings: he **blocks with both blades raised overhead**, crossed (ch. 583 pp. 7–11;
  `583Ichigo_vs._Candice`, `Ep387IchigoVsCandice`: both fists above the head, blades crossing over it). "Good thing I use
  two swords as well."
- **A small Getsuga from the short blade** cancels Galvano Javelin; anime adds **an elbow to her stomach** at close range
  (`Ep387IchigoElbowsCandice`: shoulder-first, blades held back).
- Meninas: he "fend[s] off her dumbbell blows with **both blades**", a rapid clash (`Ep387IchigoVsMeninas`: one blade swept
  low across, a glowing arc); dodges four skeleton rushes and **destroys them with a dual slash**.
- **Jūjishō** vs Electrocution (`GetsugaJujisho.gif`): 0.0–0.8 s **both blades crossed low in front of the hips**, an X of
  light where they meet; 3.1 s a **wide horizontal sweep** trailing gold; 4.4 s the long blade lifted high one-handed;
  6.0–6.3 s silhouette, **the long blade raised high** for the vertical half; 7.0 s the cross. Then he **shouts at her to
  dodge** (ch. 584).

### 1.4 The Soul King palace vs Yhwach (ch. 613–619; ep. 28 = Ep394; A)
- Touching Yhwach's sword, his right side is taken over by Blut Vene and he **cuts the Soul King diagonally** (ch. 614;
  `614Ichigo_slashes`: a low-to-high diagonal, right arm extended long).
- **Diagonal attack** at Yhwach, blocked (ch. 615; `615Ichigo_and_Yhwach_clash`).
- **Grabs Yhwach's wrist** to stop him destroying the king (ch. 618; `618Ichigo_grabs`), then **a Getsuga from the long
  blade** blasts him out of the palace (ch. 618 pp. 9–16; `618Ichigo_blasts`).
- Anime (Ep394): **the X guard** (`Ep394IchigoPreparesFight`: right fist low with the long blade upright, left fist at the
  chest with the short blade level across it, knees bent); the **Getsuga shout** (`GetsugaTensho394.gif` 0–0.6 s "Getsuga…":
  the long blade **cocked back over the right shoulder**, teeth bared; 1.0 s "…Tenshō!" he is already flying in);
  **Jūjishō vs Sankt Zwinger** (`GetsugaJujisho394.gif` 0.6–1.4 s: the long grip level across his waist, then **arms flung
  wide**, then **the short blade sweeps low and level** while **the long blade is held high behind**; 3.0 s the cross);
  **flying lunge** (`Ep394YhwachEvadesIchigo`: the body flat and forward, long blade straight up behind the head, short
  blade low and forward, the waist cloak streaming like a cape).

### 1.5 vs Uryū (ch. 619–620, ep. 30 = Ep396; ch. 659–660, ep. 40 = Ep406; A)
- Uryū **shoots his sword**; they clash; a Heilig Pfeil hits and Blut Vene stops it (Ep396; `Ep396IchigoBlutVene`).
- He **deflects** arrow volleys (`Ep396IchigoDefendsBarrage`, `…CountersBarrage`) and **slashes an arrow apart**
  (`Ep396IchigoSlashesArrow`: a wide one-armed horizontal follow-through, the right arm across the body, the **left hand
  open and flung back** for balance, knees bent low).
- **Jūjishō deliberately misses** Uryū (Ep396; `GetsugaJujisho396.gif`, `Ep396GetsugaJujisho`: he stands above the cross,
  long blade raised one-handed).
- "Raises his sword to attack Uryū" and is **shot through the torso**, falls off the palace (ch. 620).
- Ch. 660: **leaps at Uryū with the long blade raised high overhead one-handed**, the body arched back, the robe flaring
  (`660Ichigo_attacks`). Ep406: **Chain** recall of the short blade, a small Getsuga from it (`Chain406.gif`); **rams Uryū**
  shoulder-first with the long blade trailing (`Ep406IchigoSlamsUryu`).

### 1.6 Wahrwelt throne room vs Yhwach (ch. 672–680; ep. 42–47 = Ep408–413; A)
- **Long blade only at first**; slashing through the darkness he "draws the smaller sword … and begins pressing forward by
  dispersing the darkness with slashes and smaller Getsuga Tenshōs" (ch. 673, Ep408).
- **Rides Orihime's Santen Kesshun into the air** and **leaps directly at Yhwach** (Ep408).
- **Leaps high and fires a Getsuga down**; lands, **points the smaller blade** at Yhwach, then **Jūjishō** (Ep409).
- Pushes through, "**jump[s] into the air, and come[s] down on Yhwach with a point-blank Getsuga**" that gashes his shoulder
  (Ep409); "moves above him, fires a smaller Getsuga … and comes down with a Reiatsu-infused strike that produces a
  shockwave" (Ep409).
- Against hundreds of spinning discs he **braces with both blades held in front of himself** (Ep409).
- **Dual Getsugas** through a wall of darkness (`Ep409DualGetsugaTenshos`: two crossing streaks); "**swipe[s] the tendrils
  away with both blades**" (Ep409).
- Caught in a wave in midair, he "raise[s] his right arm and larger sword out of" it to cut free; "counters the next wave
  with a **swing of his smaller sword**"; "**slowing his backward slide by grinding his smaller sword along the floor**"
  (ch. 675 pp. 10–13).
- **Half-Hollow** (ch. 675–677): **sheathes the small blade**; an explosion of reiatsu clears the room; "**grabbing Yhwach's
  right hand with his left before slashing the larger sword directly at Yhwach's head**" (ch. 676 pp. 7–13;
  `676Ichigo_vs._Yhwach`, `Ep410IchigoAttacksYhwach`); thrown into the air, he **stops in midair, raises the blade**,
  charges a Getsuga and a Gran Rey Cero **from the horn**, glares with red eyes and fires the **Cero Creciente**
  (ch. 676 pp. 13–17; `CeroCreciente.gif` 9.7 s blade overhead with a gold flame at the tip, 23.1 s hanging in the air
  blade high, 24–25.6 s **landing crouched, the blade trailing up behind**).
- Yhwach's traps: impaled in the foot, **springs off a pillar**, stabbed from the wall (ch. 677).
- **Bankai**: broken at once (ch. 678); he **bats the thrown horn away** with the broken blade (Ep413).

### 1.7 血鎖の一護 vs Yhwach (anime ep. 45 = Ep411; anime-original; A from the episode page and stills)
- Before: with the larger sword he "**alternates between evading and riding atop**" Yhwach's torrents to clash in midair;
  Yhwach grabs him **by the horn** and throws him; after a beam, he **grabs Orihime's trembling wrist with his left hand**.
- Activation: the moon eclipses, "Ichigo activate[s] his Bankai just as the moon is fully eclipsed", emerges from darkness
  (`Ep411IchigoActivatesBloodChains`).
- **The chain wall**: "red Reiatsu chains … instantly extend from Ichigo's sword and form a protective wall that deflects or
  redirects all the beams" (`Ep411IchigoChainsDeflectBeams`; `BloodChains.gif` 13.6–16.1 s: a net of chains, **the blade
  held up diagonally before his face**).
- **The walk**: he walks toward Yhwach leaving black-flame footprints, then "runs toward him fast enough to disappear from
  sight and kick up a shockwave", "**reappears directly in front of him and slashes diagonally**"; Yhwach steps back and is
  **cut twice** (the delayed second cut).
- **Clones**: "the Ichigo in front … disperses into black Reiatsu while another Ichigo jumps high into the air behind him …
  comes down on him with a **powerful overhead swing**", a third repeats it **from above** and cuts through Yhwach and his
  sword; a fourth "preparing to attack from above". The real one cuts through a point-blank beam with his sword, is pushed
  back meters, **runs back in** dodging beams.
- A clone "alternates between **evading and cutting through**" beams and "**land[s] in front of Orihime, defending her from
  a dozen energy beams at once with his sword**" (`Ep411CloneProtectsOrihime`: blade held up before him).
- "The real Ichigo standing with and generating more shadowy Reiatsu clones **by moving across the battlefield**"; Ichigo
  and the clones rush in, launch Yhwach into the air with **a combined attack**, more clones hit him with **flying strikes**
  and **suspend him in midair with thick strands of Reiatsu**.
- **The black Getsuga**: "from directly above Yhwach, Ichigo falls, gathers the dark Reiatsu around his sword, and unleashes
  a Getsuga Tenshō … a massive black crescent moon"; he "**floats between the two points of the crescent**" with red eyes
  (`Ep411GetsugaTensho`; `BloodChainsGetsugaTensho411.gif` 18–33 s the crescent, 34.3–36.3 s him floating in the gap,
  blade low, chain scarf streaming). The clones are reabsorbed into the sword for it (Reiatsu Clones page).
- Beams **curve away from his edge**; he **leaps backward**. Then "rapidly moving through the air around Yhwach in multiple
  directions to spawn dozens of clones, Ichigo has them **fall onto Yhwach**", **cuts through the darkness from behind**, a
  **point-blank Cero Creciente** (the form keeps it).
- **The bind**: "Ichigo **slides back** from Yhwach and **wraps the latter in his Reiatsu chains** to restrain him" while
  Rukia freezes him, then moves close for a **second point-blank Getsuga**. He still loses: Yhwach stands on his head.

### 1.8 Ep. 46 (Ep412) and the end (ch. 681–684; ep. 47–48 = Ep413–414; A)
- Ep412: he **attacks with the larger blade**, it is blocked, he **pulls out the smaller blade to slash**; Yhwach answers
  with a second broadsword ("the only one who can dual-wield"). Before the Bankai: **draws the smaller blade**, flames flare
  at his feet, a **pillar of flame** from Zangetsu, the eclipse, he "merges his two swords into a larger one".
- Restored Bankai: `Ep414TrueTensaZangetsuB`: **two-handed, low, point down-forward**, feet wide. Runs on Reishi
  footholds through the portal.
- **Impales Yhwach from behind**, then a **point-blank Getsuga** that bifurcates him (ch. 683 pp. 15–17;
  `683Ichigo_attacks`: a one-armed upward thrust from below).
- The Bankai is **bashed out of his hand**; at Uryū's shot he **leaps** with the cracked Bankai, the shell crumbles to the
  old cleaver, and he **cuts Yhwach in two** with a level cut, ending behind him (ch. 684; `684Ichigo_bifurcates`).

## 2. Games

### 2.1 BLEACH Rebirth of Souls (2025): 黒崎 一護 [千年血戦篇], DLC 1 (B)
Official JP page (bn-ent.net) and the wiki's Other Media (in-game EN names and move descriptions):
- 「近/中距離の手数を多く持ち、緩急をつけた戦いが得意」: many hits at close/mid range, a fighter of **tempo changes**.
- **Unique Flash Attack, Mid-Stance Thrust**: a dash and **a thrust with the larger blade**.
- **己有技 朔望 (Syzygy)**: 「前方に刀を向けて構えを取り」, "points the smaller blade of Zangetsu forward"; follow-ups:
  **Full Moon** (a lunge, a short flurry of slashes), **Moonlight** (a pounce, **a double overhead slam with both blades**),
  **Getsuga Tensho** (**a horizontal slash with the smaller blade** firing a golden crescent), **Rising Moon** (a dash
  forward; again = a dash back).
- **霊圧技1 双月乱舞 Sogetsu Ranbu**: charges, **a double horizontal slash**, on contact a barrage, **finished with an X slash
  from both blades**; projectile-invincible start.
- **霊圧技2 生まれ変わった力 Reborn Power**: a stance and **one level sweep**; a whiff fires Getsuga, a hit turns into Jūjishō.
- **Kikon, Getsuga Jūjishō**: "**swings the smaller blade horizontally, suspending a crescent** … **steps forward, raises
  the larger blade over his head, and brings it down** to fire another crescent, fusing both".
- **Awakening 虚化**: the long blade turns white in a golden torrent; Kikon **Gran Rey Cero from the tip of the horn**, and
  **Cero Creciente**: "raises the larger blade … roars as he **aims his horn towards the tip of the blade**".
- No 血鎖 form in RoS (it predates ep. 45). The repo's L stance 月待 is modelled on 朔望 [U, v2].

## 4. The most useful canon beats for strike clips (each a separate silhouette)
1. **Short-blade point and flick** (ch. 583, Ep409, RoS 朔望): side-on, left arm extended, the short blade aimed at the
   chest; a small wrist flick out-and-back fires the little Getsuga. Long blade held low behind. [A/B]
2. **Wide one-armed horizontal with the long blade** (`Ep396IchigoSlashesArrow`): deep knee bend, the right arm sweeps across
   to the far side, the left hand open and thrown back, the waist cloak flaring. [A]
3. **Rising diagonal** (ch. 614 the Soul King cut; Ep411 the KESSA diagonal): low-left to high-right, the arm fully long. [A]
4. **Leap and one-armed overhead drop** (ch. 660; Ep409 point-blank Getsuga; Ep411 clones): body arched back in the air,
   the long blade straight up behind the head, then down through. [A]
5. **Flying lunge** (`Ep394YhwachEvadesIchigo`): body level, long blade high behind, short blade low ahead, the cloak a
   streamer: a dash or a K-ender silhouette. [A]
6. **Crossed overhead block** (ch. 583) and **the X guard** (Ep394): a guard pose, or a cross-link's start. [A]
7. **Jūjishō in two beats** (`GetsugaJujisho.gif`, `…394.gif`, RoS Kikon): blades crossed low → arms flung wide → short
   blade level sweep (hold) → step in, long blade overhead → down; the cross forms. [A/B]
8. **Getsuga shout windup** (`GetsugaTensho394.gif`): long blade cocked back over the right shoulder, teeth bared, then the
   swing as he flies. [A]
9. **Left hand grabs the wrist, right hand cuts at the head** (ch. 618, 676): a throw / command-grab silhouette. [A]
10. **Landing crouch with the blade trailing**, or **grinding the short blade on the floor** to stop a slide (ch. 675,
    `CeroCreciente.gif`): recovery and pushback poses. [A]
KESSA extras: **the walk then vanishing diagonal that cuts twice**; **blade raised diagonally before the face as the chain
net spreads** (the parry); **slide back and bind with chains**; **from directly above, the black Getsuga**, then floating
in the crescent's gap. [A, anime]

## 5. Gaps
- **Frame timing**: the GIFs are 190×108 and 13–66 s cuts; windup/active ratios are not measurable. Close: watch Ep387,
  Ep394, Ep396, Ep409–Ep411 at full resolution; trace, never redistribute.
- **Manga black-and-white pages** (ch. 583, 615, 660, 673–677, 683–684) were not read page by page; the battle pages'
  prose and the wiki's colour panels stand in.
- **RoS animations**: only names and one-line descriptions; the actual RoS TYBW Ichigo clips (Syzygy follow-ups, Sogetsu
  Ranbu, the Jūjishō Kikon, the half-Hollow Cero Creciente) need a gameplay video.
- **Ep411 choreography between the listed beats** (how each clone's swing reads, the chain parry's exact pose) is from the
  episode page's prose and red-graded 190 px GIFs; the chain wall pose is a guess from `BloodChains.gif` 14–16 s.
- **Short-blade parry**: no source makes the short blade a dedicated guard; if the kit wants it, it is our design.
- **Brave Souls** animations: not documented in text.
- Not researched: the Ōken-clothing descent (ch. 555, Ep385) motion and the Askin fight (off-panel loss, ch. 648–656).
