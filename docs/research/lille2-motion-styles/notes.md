# Strongly stylised fighting-game moves: notes for Lille II's awakened stance (2026-10-11)

The user (verbatim): 「長鞭可以連成大砍刀，砲擊也可以接在近距離毆打後面，請調查一般格鬥遊戲/動漫風格對戰遊戲常會有強烈風格的戰鬥動作，並選擇適合的搭配來建置動作模組」.
Web searches (2026-10-11); the sources are trope catalogues, wikis, guides and patch notes, so these are examples of

## Recurring archetypes

| Archetype | Examples | What makes it read |
|---|---|---|
| **Snake sword: whip ↔ blade** | Ivy Valentine's segmented blade (SoulCalibur): a sword that splits into a whip for reach and snaps back into a blade; her moves change with the blade's state, strings cross between the states (e.g. a whip move into a sword move) | the length change itself: the stretch out, the snap back to a rigid edge |
| **Rush → blast** | Dragon Ball FighterZ's auto combo ends in a super with enough meter; Sparking! ZERO's rush chain (up to 4 hits) then a smash; a Gotenks fan movelist's flurry punch then a charged punch that explodes on impact; DMC's Stinger → Million Stab (a rush into a flurry) | many quick close hits, then one point-blank release with a big effect and knock-back |
| **Spinning tornado (multi-hit)** | Street Fighter's Tatsumaki Senpukyaku / Hurricane Kick (a spinning multi-hit, 3 hits in its early versions); Guilty Gear's Bandit Revolver (a lunging spin of kicks, likened to the Tatsu) | the body as a rotor, each hit as a limb passes the front |
| **Restraint, then the finisher** | Gurren Lagann pins with the boomerang before the Giga Drill Breaker; Combattler V stuns with Choudenji Tatsumaki before the Choudenji Spin ram; DMC Round Trip locking enemies before a follow-up | a light setup hit that "holds" him, then the heavy one |
| **Heavy overhead commitment** | DMC's Helm Breaker (a falling vertical slash); Guilty Gear's Bandit Bringer (an overhead, hurled at him) | a long wind-up held, the whole body behind one chop, a heavy landing |
| **Simultaneous flurry** | Rurouni Kenshin's Kuzu Ryu Sen (nine slashes that look simultaneous); Cloud's Omnislash | hits too fast to count, a burst of afterimages |

## The choice for Lille II's awakened stance (JILLIEL EN; the owl EN on its claws / gold wings)

The three opener strings keep their roles and frame data (L 回收 even beat, J 回復 rapid, K 高攻 heavy); each gets one
archetype, and the user's two combinations are built in as the strings' finales:

- **J (rapid) = the snake sword**: J1 / J2 the light wings stretched into whips, lashing (crossing figure-eights); **J3's
  lashes snap rigid into one giant cleaver** (both wings joined) for its last hit, a heavy downward chop
  (「長鞭連成大砍刀」). Reach: the whips ~2.6–3.0 m, the cleaver ~2.4 m.
- **K (heavy) = the great sword, then rush → blast**: K1 the great-sword chops (slow, heavy, Helm-Breaker-like overhead then
  a rising cut); K2 stepping in, a close beating (two heavy blows with the wings' roots at ~1.2–1.4 m); **K3 two close blows
  then the point-blank cannon blast with an explosion** (「砲擊也可以接在近距離毆打後面」).
- **L (even beat) = the spinning top**: tilted forward, the wings swung flat, three turns over the string (L1, L2 one each,
  L3 one and a half), each hit as a wing sweeps the front (~1.8 m).

The spending L L follow-ups (§8.5's 疾射 / 重砲 lines) stay as they are.
